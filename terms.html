You own what you draw. By submitting a drawing to a round you give us a licence to show it inside the app to other players, including during voting and on the results screen. Practice drawings you save are shown only to friends you have accepted.

3. Zero tolerance for objectionable content

There is no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive behaviour. Do not draw or submit anything that is sexual, violent, hateful, harassing, illegal, or that targets a real person. Drawings are screened automatically before they can become finalists, any player can report a drawing or block another player, and we review every report within 24 hours. We remove content that breaks these rules and we close accounts that keep breaking them.

4. Fair play

One account per person. Do not vote with multiple accounts, automate the app, or interfere with other players' rounds.

5. Points

Points are a game score with no cash value. We may adjust or reset scores to fix abuse or bugs.

6. Ending your account

You can delete your account at any time from Settings → Delete account. That permanently removes your profile, points, drawings and friendships. We may suspend or delete accounts that break these terms.
