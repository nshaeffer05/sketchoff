Common questions

When is the round? A random time each day, never before 8am or after midnight in your US time zone. Turn notifications on so you don't miss it.

I missed the drawing window. You can still vote if voting is open. Otherwise, tomorrow.

How are finalists chosen? Every drawing is checked for how well it matches the prompt. Five are picked at random from the ones that do.

Reporting a drawing or blocking someone. Open the drawing and tap Report, or open a friend's profile and tap the ••• menu to block. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours.

Deleting your account. Settings → Delete account. It is immediate and permanent.
