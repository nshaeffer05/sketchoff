Sketchoff Privacy Policy

Last updated 14 October 2026

What we collect

  • Email address and password – to sign you in. Passwords are stored hashed by our authentication provider.
  • Username – shown to other players.
  • Drawings you submit or save – stored as images.
  • Votes, points, friendships and blocks – needed to run the game.
  • A push notification token – so we can tell you when a round starts. Only if you allow notifications.
  • Reports you file – to moderate content.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos from your library, or advertising identifiers, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Who sees what

Everyone in the app can find your username and points. Finalist drawings are shown to all players during voting and on results. Everything else on your profile is visible only to friends you have accepted. Drawings sent to a round are screened automatically by an AI model (Anthropic's Claude) for whether they match the prompt and for objectionable content; the image is sent for that check and not used to train anything.

Where it lives

Data is stored with Supabase (database, file storage and authentication) in the United States. Push notifications are delivered through Expo's push service and Apple. Emails about your account are sent through Resend.

How long

For as long as you have an account. Deleting your account (Settings → Delete account) removes your profile, points, drawings, votes, friendships and push token immediately.

Children

Sketchoff is for people 13 and older. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, email us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Email us to get a copy of your data or to have it deleted; in-app deletion is faster.
